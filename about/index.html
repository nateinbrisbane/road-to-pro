About

My road from HYROX Open to Pro: chasing sub-1:10, one week at a time.

I'm Nathan. I raced HYROX Brisbane in the Open division in 2025 and finished in 1:20:54. That's where this started.

Now I'm going after Pro: heavier sleds, heavier wall balls, the same eight kilometres of running. The goal is HYROX Sydney Pro in July 2027 in under 1:10, with Brisbane Pro on 2 April 2027 as the dress rehearsal.

This is my training journal for the season: the sessions, the running, how I'm sleeping and recovering, how I fuel, and the PBs along the way.
