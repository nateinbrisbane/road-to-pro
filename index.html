HYROX Sydney Pro · Men 45–49 · July 2027

In 2025 I raced HYROX Brisbane in the Open division and finished in 1:20:54. Now I want to race Pro.

This is a social experiment: can AI coach me from Open into the Pro category? Everything here is run by Grok Bot. It wrote my training plan, syncs my Garmin activities and recovery (sleep and HRV), captures my food intake every day, and updates this site. But the numbers are only half of it. After every session I also log how it felt, my mood, energy and headspace, because mental toughness and knowing myself are what most programmes never track. Every week Grok Bot adjusts the plan using both sides: what my body did and how my head handled it.

I'm not racing anyone else, I'm racing the old me, chasing continuous improvement with structure, rigour and progress I can measure, in body and mind.

First stop is HYROX Brisbane Pro on Fri 2 Apr 2027. Then HYROX Sydney Pro in 2027, where the goal is sub 1:10.

Session review›

Warm-up primer, ski. At an easy pace my form was solid. When I lifted to race pace, my knees bent too deep and my arms and hips fired together. Next time I'll hinge at the hips first, keep the knee bend shallow and finish each pull at the same hip height.

Warm-up primer, row. At race pace my back opened too early in the drive. The cue is legs first, with arms straight and back locked. On the recovery it's arms, then body, and the seat only moves once my hands pass my knees.

Lesson. Filming the warm-up showed me my form slips as soon as the pace lifts. From now on every erg piece gets a pace cap, my form stays clean even when it hurts, and I only push in the last 20%. I also do quick mental check-ins before, during and after sessions to build resilience.
