HYROX Brisbane Pro · Men 45–49 · Fri 2 Apr 2027

In 2025 I raced HYROX Brisbane in the Open division and finished in 1:20:54. Now I want to race Pro.

This is a social experiment: can AI coach me from Open into the Pro category? Everything here is run by Grok Bot. It wrote my training plan, syncs my Garmin activities and recovery (sleep and HRV), captures my food intake every day, and updates this site. But the numbers are only half of it. After every session I also log how it felt, my mood, energy and headspace, because mental toughness and knowing myself are what most programmes never track. Every week Grok Bot adjusts the plan using both sides: what my body did and how my head handled it.

I'm not racing anyone else, I'm racing the old me, chasing continuous improvement with structure, rigour and progress I can measure, in body and mind.

The main race is HYROX Brisbane Pro on Fri 2 Apr 2027, where the goal is sub 1:10. Later in 2027 I'll race HYROX Sydney in Mixed Doubles.

Sun15 Nov Controlled run-to-station brickFresh burpee broad jumps 3×10 m; 3×1 km run into pull, carry or wall-ball work; 5 km total 5 km ›
  • 1 km easy warm-up (at least 10 minutes). While fresh: burpee broad jumps 3×10 m, 60–90 s rest, economical repeatable jumps; no maximal distance.
  • 3 rounds, each starting with 1 km controlled run at RPE 5–6, breathing settled before station. Round 1: turf rope/plate pull 3×10 m at 40 kg plates (30 kg when technique requires it), 45 s between lengths; controlled backward steps only where safe. Round 2: farmers carry 3×30 m at 2×30 kg DBs (2×28 kg if unavailable), 30 s between carries, stop short of grip failure. Round 3: wall balls 3×10 at 5.5 kg with deliberate 20–30 s breaks; then DB thrusters 2×8 at 2×5 kg, 60 s rest. These are separate skill/load substitutes, not equivalent to a 9 kg ball.
  • Rest 2 min between rounds, longer until breathing controlled when technique requires it. No rigid run pace; slow the next run if station form deteriorates. Finish with 1 km easy cool-down; 5 km total + 10 min mobility. No full-distance station tests or extra Pro day pass.
