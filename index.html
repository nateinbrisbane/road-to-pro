HYROX Brisbane Pro · Men 45–49 · Fri 2 Apr 2027

In 2025 I raced HYROX Brisbane in the Open division and finished in 1:20:54. Now I want to race Pro.

This is a social experiment: can AI coach me from Open into the Pro category? Everything here is run by Grok Bot. It wrote my training plan, syncs my Garmin activities and recovery (sleep and HRV), captures my food intake every day, and updates this site. But the numbers are only half of it. After every session I also log how it felt, my mood, energy and headspace, because mental toughness and knowing myself are what most programmes never track. Every week Grok Bot adjusts the plan using both sides: what my body did and how my head handled it.

I'm not racing anyone else, I'm racing the old me, chasing continuous improvement with structure, rigour and progress I can measure, in body and mind.

The main race is HYROX Brisbane Pro on Fri 2 Apr 2027, where the goal is sub 1:10. Later in 2027 I'll race HYROX Sydney in Mixed Doubles.

Session review›

Back squat. Depth is above parallel, with the hip crease about 3–4 cm above the knee. Hips rise first and the bar drifts forward, worst in set 1; sets 3–4 were cleaner. Cues: narrow grip, shoulder blades squeezed, elbows down; a natural "squatting in the woods" stance, toes slightly out; belly breath held until back up. Aim 3–5 cm deeper.

Wall balls. 30 unbroken on the filmed set, with a good high catch riding into the squat. Fixes: squat deeper so the hip is clearly below the knee (no-rep risk), keep feet planted (currently a 4–6 cm hop), and stand with toes 40–50 cm from the wall (currently about 90 cm).

Session review›

Trap-bar deadlift at 90 kg, last working set from the side: all 8 reps clean, with a neutral back, a vertical bar path, full lockout and a dead stop each rep. Reps 7–8 are clearly the weakest: at lift-off the chest is lower (torso about 40° from horizontal vs about 49° on rep 1) and the eyes drop to the floor. Cue: chest up with eyes about 2 m ahead, pull the slack out before the bar moves, then push the floor away so hips and shoulders rise together. Weight stays at 90 kg until the last reps hold their shape.
